The Cognitive Funnel: How an Intelligent Mind Solves Problems

Moving from broad observation, to reframing the core problem, to evaluating options, to picking the one best path.

1
REFLECT Wide Aperture
💬 "Wait, what is actually happening right now?"
The Action: Pause impulsive action. Put your ego and guesses aside. Observe raw data and audit past attempts without defensiveness.
2
REFRAME Problem Filter
💬 "What is the single real problem we need to solve?"
The Action: Strip away historical baggage and conventional assumptions. Narrow the focus from surface symptoms down to the root objective.
3
ANALYZE Option Dissection
💬 "What are the viable options, costs & trade-offs?"
The Action: Dissect the problem into candidate solutions. Measure dependencies, calculate pros and cons, and stress-test every alternative.
4
SYNTHESIZE Narrowest Choice
💬 "Which single option do we pick, and why does it win?"
The Action: Choose the single best option from your analysis. State the exact reason why it beats the alternatives, align the team, and execute.
