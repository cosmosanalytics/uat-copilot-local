Call Center Multi-Agent Hierarchy

How specialized AI agents coordinate synchronously during a live customer voice call.

4 Sub-Agents • 1 Supervisor

Lead Orchestrator & Supervisor Agent

Root Tier-1

Manages caller state machine, delegates sub-tasks, enforces global SLA & business goals.

State: Active
Tools: Session Context Manager • Sub-Agent Bus • Fallback Router P99 Latency: 25ms

1. Voice Concierge Agent

Inbound Speech & Intent Classifier
Streaming Turn

Manages real-time bi-directional voice streaming, caller greeting, ANI/PIN identity authentication, and intent extraction.

Input: Live Audio Stream Output: Structured Intent Payload

2. SOP Policy & Knowledge Agent

Knowledge Graph (KG) Rulebook Evaluator
Rule Engine

Queries the official SOP Knowledge Graph for allowed policy actions, financial limits, and exception conditions (e.g. max $150 credit).

Input: Policy Query Output: Validated Boundary Rules

3. Action & CRM Execution Agent

Transactional Tool Calling
Read / Write

Interacts with backend systems: executes bill adjustments, dispatches field technician tickets, or issues customer credits.

Input: Action Directive Output: API Confirmation / ID

4. QA & Business Graph Agent

Call Record (CG) & Goals (BG) Evaluator
Post-Call Audit

Synthesizes the Context Graph call diary, scores compliance against SOP rules, and calculates business ROI and AHT/FCR metrics.

Input: Completed Dialogue Output: Context & Business Graph
Execution Pipeline:
Customer Utterance → Voice Concierge → SOP Policy → Action Engine → QA & Business Graph
