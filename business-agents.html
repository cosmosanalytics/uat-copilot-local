Agent hierarchy: a business run by agents

You are the board of a small online store. Customers place purchase orders, and everything else follows from them. Give a directive and watch it flow down a hierarchy of agents, then watch the reports flow back up while the business console changes.

  1. Customers: Place the orders. They are the external input. Late orders and refunds come back as support tickets, which are the only thing in the support inbox.
  2. Executive agent: Reads your directive and sets the company stance, which fixes the limits below it.
  3. Department managers: Sales, Operations, Support and Finance each choose a plan within those limits.
  4. Workers: A pricing desk and an inventory planner (code), a ticket triager and reply drafter (model), and a refund processor (code) do the work and report up.
  5. You: The human at the top. You can override the stance.

Honest label: the store, its customers, orders, tickets and numbers are made up, and the scoring is illustrative. The language model makes the executive and manager choices from short menus, sorts tickets, writes short replies and reports. Pricing, inventory planning and refunds are plain code. Small models make mistakes, and the limits flowing down from the executive are there to contain them. This is a teaching demo of delegation and reporting, not a business tool. Nothing leaves your device.
