An art team: from brief to variants

Give the team a one-line brief. A director sets the palette and composition, three concept artists with different lenses propose ideas, a prompt engineer turns each idea into a prompt, an image backend draws it, and code measures the result. A critic that sees only numbers finds what misses the brief, a revision fixes it, and a curator ranks the variants. You make the final pick.

  1. Creative director: Turns the brief into a palette, a composition, and things to include and avoid.
  2. Three concept artists: A surrealist, a minimalist and a classicist, so the ideas differ.
  3. Prompt engineer: Turns each concept into a prompt and settings for the image backend.
  4. Image backend: A procedural canvas that works everywhere, or SD-Turbo on WebGPU (experimental).
  5. Critic and measurements: Code measures palette match, contrast, balance and busyness. The critic sees only the numbers.
  6. Curator: Ranks the variants by measured score. You make the final pick.

Honest label: the agents are a small on-device chat model, so some replies cannot be read, and the page then falls back to simple rules and says so. The procedural canvas draws simple generative art from the plan. It is not an AI image model. The SD-Turbo backend is a best-effort adapter that I could not test, so treat it as experimental. The measurements are plain pixel statistics that check the image against the brief. They do not measure beauty, so your own pick is the real test. The model weights download from huggingface.co. Nothing you type leaves your device.
