A team of AI agents with different jobs plans, cuts, scores and critiques a short film (WebLLM agents, a generated animatic)

A movie crew w knowledge graph: from brief to a screened short

Give the crew a one-line brief. A producer sets the plan, three screenwriters with different lenses write treatments, a cinematographer picks camera moves, and a composer picks the music. Plain code cuts each treatment into a timeline, draws every shot and measures the cut. A critic that sees only numbers finds what weakens it, an editor fixes it, and a curator ranks the cuts. Then you watch them in a screening room, pick one, and can record it.

  1. Producer: Turns the brief into a title, logline, tone and palette.
  2. Three screenwriters: Classic three-act, twist ending and visual poem, so the stories differ.
  3. Cinematographer and composer: Camera moves and framing for each shot, and a generated score.
  4. Editor and measurements: Code cuts the timeline and measures runtime, pacing, color jumps, mood, payoff and captions.
  5. Critic and curator: A critic that sees only the numbers, then a ranking by measured score. You make the final pick.

Honest label: this is an animatic, not AI video. The shots are simple generated scenes with camera moves, captions and a generated score. The agents are a small on-device chat model, so some replies cannot be read, and the page then falls back to simple rules and says so. The measurements check craft rules such as runtime, pacing and continuity. They do not measure whether the film is good, so your own pick is the real test. Recording uses your browser's video recorder and may not work in every browser. Nothing leaves your device.
