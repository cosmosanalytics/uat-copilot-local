2Knowledge base

Documentation about expected UI behavior — functional specs, help text, known-good states. Each chunk is quality-checked and must be approved before it can ground an assessment. When you assess a step, the app automatically finds the most relevant approved chunks for that step and includes them in what the AI is shown, so its judgment is checked against your documented expectations, not just general appearance.

AI assistance for reviewers — drafts and pre-screens, never approves

The AI can draft a documentation chunk from step-assessment history, and can pre-screen pending chunks for clarity (5-sample self-consistency — this is a text model, so unlike the vision worker this signal is real) and for apparent contradiction with already-approved chunks. Neither of those is an approval — every chunk still needs a named person's sign-off. The pre-screen just tells that person which chunks are safe to approve quickly and which need a closer look.
