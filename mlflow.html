MLflow tracking and registry (real scikit-learn, MLflow-style tracker)

MLflow lab

Train real models in your browser, log every run with parameters, metrics and artifacts, then compare runs, chart them and register the best one. The tracker has MLflow's method names and stores runs in SQLite tables.

Honest label: the models are real (scikit-learn running in your browser) but MLflow itself is not, because it needs a tracking server. The tracker here mirrors mlflow.start_run, log_param, log_metric and log_artifact so the code you read transfers directly. The registry and stage names follow MLflow's model registry concepts.
