🪑 1. THE DESK: Working Memory & Attention Gating Baddeley • Cowan (~4 Chunks) • Lost-in-the-Middle
🧠 Upgrade Your Brain
►Chunk to 3-4 items: You only hold ~4 chunks. Group chaotic to-dos into 3 bold themes per day.
►Clear the clutter: 50 open browser tabs silently dilute your prefrontal focus. Push them off the desk.
►Top-down spotlight: Aim attention at your goal. Silence bottom-up notification grabs (pagers/pings).
🤖 Upgrade Your AI Agent
►Desk, not warehouse: Context is not an attic. Strip intermediate logs; cap scratchpad at <2k tokens.
►Pin at the poles: Put the core goal at the start and key rules at the end. Dodge the middle blind spot.
►Mask unneeded tools: Hide irrelevant tool manifests during sub-tasks to stop prompt distraction.
📖 2. THE DIARY & THE SHELF: Grounding Facts with Receipts Tulving • Hippocampus vs Cortex • Recency + Relevance
🧠 Upgrade Your Brain
►Log surprises immediately: Human memory drifts and rebuilds. Write 2-line notes right after surprises.
►Attach source receipts: When learning facts, attach who/when/where. A fact without a scene is hearsay.
►Combat false memory: Check original written notes rather than trusting confident intuition.
🤖 Upgrade Your AI Agent
►Log first, summarize later: Never replace raw run logs with lossy summaries; logs are ground truth.
►Vector + Graph receipts: Require a verifiable citation (source URL + chunk hash) before returning facts.
►Retrieve by 3 signals: Search by relevance + recency + importance, never by cosine similarity alone.
🌙 3. THE SLEEP COMPILER: Turning Practice into Autopilot Habits Basal Ganglia • Offline Replay • Gated SKILL.md
🧠 Upgrade Your Brain
►Protect slow-wave sleep: Your hippocampus replays daytime lessons at night to bake them into habits.
►Deliberate practice with feedback: Autopilot habits are rigid; only conscious critique prevents bad routines.
►Spacing over cramming: Three 30-min spaced sessions consolidate far deeper than one 5-hour marathon.
🤖 Upgrade Your AI Agent
►Consolidate offline behind gates: Run nightly cron jobs on failure logs to draft candidate SKILL.md runbooks.
►Writing habits is high risk: A wrong skill runs every time! Only compile stable, tested, repeated routines.
►Regression test suite: Rerun 20 known-answer cases before publishing any skill. Zero regressions allowed.
🔮 4. THE FORK: Fast vs. Slow Mode & Pre-Mortem Rehearsal Kahneman System 1/2 • Klein Pre-Mortem • Dry-Run Sandbox
🧠 Upgrade Your Brain
►Pause on obvious hunches: Catch the "bat-and-ball" trap. When an answer feels fast, engage 5s of System 2.
►Run a 60-second Pre-Mortem: Before launching: "It failed horribly 6 months from now. Why?" Add a guard.
►Predict before checking: Guess the answer before googling. The surprise delta is where learning lives.
🤖 Upgrade Your AI Agent
►Fast by default, slow on purpose: Route trivial queries to <30ms System 1; route state mutations to System 2.
►Dry-run sandbox before writes: Run destructive actions in shadow mode or plan mode before touching real APIs.
►Expectation assertion: State the expected outcome before calling a tool; abort if observation clashes.
👁️ 5. THE HONEST WATCHER: Calibrated Confidence & Saying "I Don't Know" Nelson & Narens • Miyake Core Three • Escalation Thresholds
🧠 Upgrade Your Brain
►Calibrate your ego: People are notoriously overconfident. Treat a 90% feeling of knowing as a cue to verify.
►Reward 'I don't know': Admitting ignorance unlocks research. Faking certainty leads to catastrophic blunders.
►Inhibit impulses (The Pause): Strengthen the prefrontal muscle: stop automatic reflexes before they speak.
🤖 Upgrade Your AI Agent
►Discount self-reported confidence: Stated confidence is generated text. Calibrate thresholds from empirical data.
►Reward abstention: Make "I do not know" an allowed answer. If confidence dips or stakes are high, escalate to human.
►Ring-0 Inhibitory Gate: Intercept hallucinations, credential leaks, and raw shell drops at the boundary.
THE UNIFIED LAW OF COGNITIVE DESIGN:
Treat the context window as a desk (not an attic). Consolidate messy daytime logs into tested nighttime habits. Rehearse irreversible actions in a sandbox. And above all, reward calibrated uncertainty over fluent, confident hallucination.
