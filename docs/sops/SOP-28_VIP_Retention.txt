GOVERNANCE: ENTERPRISE REVENUE PROTECTION COMMITTEE

1. PURPOSE & SCOPE
Defines contract renegotiation, discount ceilings, and multi-year extension terms for
accounts with Annual Contract Value (ACV) exceeding $10,000.00 USD.

2. RETENTION CEILINGS
2.1 Standard Account Representatives:
    - Maximum allowable discount on renewal: 5.0% without director sign-off.
2.2 Executive Clause 3.1: High-Value Retention Allowance:
    - Applicable only to accounts with verified ARR > $15,000.00 USD.
    - Tier-1 Enterprise Specialists are authorized to offer up to 15.0% contract rate reduction
      contingent on executing a 24-month contract lock.
    - Financial Justification: Retaining $18,000 ARR with $2,700 concession yields +$15,300 net
      annual retained value, preventing churn to competitors.
