1. PURPOSE & SCOPE
Defines diagnostic sequences for residential fiber optical line terminal (ONT) and
gateway faults prior to dispatching field technical personnel ("truck rolls").

2. MANDATORY DIAGNOSTIC SEQUENCE
2.1 Telemetry Verification:
    - Agent must query the Area Optical Loop Telemetry.
    - If Red Optical Alarm (Loss of Signal / LOS) is confirmed on the neighborhood hub,
      remote modem reboot sequences are strictly counter-indicated.
2.2 Step-by-Step Diagnostic Verification:
    - Step 1: Query Hub Telemetry (Verify upstream attenuation).
    - Step 2: In-Home Optical Cable Inspection (Direct customer to inspect green SC/APC connector).
    - Step 3: Hardware Dispatch Decision:
      * Standard truck roll fee: $120.00 USD.
      * Clause 6.1 (Hardware Defect Waiver): If LOS is confirmed on upstream telemetry,
        the dispatch fee is 100% waived, and an urgent field technician slot is scheduled within 4 hours.
