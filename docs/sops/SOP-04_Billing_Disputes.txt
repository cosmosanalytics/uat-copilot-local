TITLE: TELECOM BILLING DISPUTES & INTERNATIONAL ROAMING WAIVERS
CLASSIFICATION: INTERNAL COMPLIANCE - LEVEL 2
EFFECTIVE DATE: 2026-01-15
GOVERNANCE: CONSUMER OPERATIONS POLICY COMMITTEE
================================================================================

1. PURPOSE & SCOPE
This document establishes the mandated operational parameters for Customer Service
Representatives (CSRs) regarding billing fee disputes, with specific governance
for international data/voice roaming assessments.

2. STANDARD AUTHORIZATION THRESHOLDS
2.1 Tier-1 Customer Care Representatives:
    - Empowered to grant billing concessions up to a hard ceiling of $50.00 USD per billing cycle.
    - Concessions between $50.01 and $200.00 USD strictly require Supervisory escalation (Form SUP-B9).
2.2 Tier-2 Advanced Support:
    - Authority ceiling up to $200.00 USD.

3. SECTION 4.2: VIP CUSTOMER HARDSHIP & RETENTION CLAUSE (EXCEPTION PROVISION)
3.1 Qualifications:
    - The customer must hold an active account lifetime value (LTV) exceeding $1,500.00 USD.
    - Zero prior roaming fee concessions in the preceding 12 months (clean dispute history).
    - Account status must be in Good Standing (no active collections).
3.2 Authority Grant:
    - Tier-1 CSRs are granted expanded discretionary authority up to $250.00 USD waiver limit
      WITHOUT requiring supervisor escalation, provided the sole objective is immediate First
      Contact Resolution (FCR) and customer retention.
3.3 Mandatory Audit Logging:
    - The agent must record the exact justification code "VIP_HARDSHIP_4.2" in the CRM billing ledger.
