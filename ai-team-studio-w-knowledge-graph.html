Design an AI team and test it, in simulation or with a real on-device model

AI team design studio w knowledge graph

Design a team of AI agents the way the executive brief says to: choose its structure, its incentives and five rules, see the team drawn as a graph, then test the design on refund cases whose answers are known. Compare designs in a notebook, and prove them against one agent.

  1. Structure (graph theory): Whether there is a checker, what flows on each edge, and whether the judge is one agent or three clones.
  2. Incentives (game theory): The checker's goal, and how often it audits.
  3. Five rules: Different eyes, check then act, facts from tools, allowed to say unsure, and prove it.
  4. Test bench: Run a design on generated cases, in simulation (instant) or with the language model (real, slow).
  5. Notebook: Every run is kept, so you can compare designs side by side.

Honest label: the refund policy and cases are made up and generated by code, and the right answers are computed by code. In simulated mode the agents are not models: their error rates are assumptions that encode the brief's claims, so the simulation shows the logic of the design rules, not how any real model performs. The assumptions are listed on the page. In language-model mode a small on-device model does the work, results are noisy, and small samples prove little. Nothing leaves your device.
