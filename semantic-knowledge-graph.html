🕸️ Semantic Knowledge Graph

Turn any text into a graph of meaning. Sentences become nodes, semantic similarity becomes edges, and graphology with Louvain community detection finds the natural clusters, the same algorithm used for community detection in large-scale network analysis. Embeddings come from a small model running locally in your browser.
