🕸️ Semantic Knowledge Graph

Turn any text into a graph of meaning. Sentences become nodes, semantic similarity becomes edges. Two four-part scales govern everything that happens next.

Level of thinking how much effort goes into an answer

  1. Reflex — one instant guess
  2. Deliberation — reasons step by step
  3. Metacognition — drafts, then checks itself once
  4. Planning — breaks into steps, stops for a tool call (e.g. calculator, search) every few steps to stay grounded and reset drift

Level of check how rigorously that answer gets verified

  1. Coverage — does the graph even know this?
  2. Contradiction — does anything disagree?
  3. Confidence — does it agree with itself, 5 times?
  4. Stakes-Adjusted Risk — is that good enough to trust here?

Neither scale wants to run at full depth by default. Too little thinking gives shallow, incompetent guesses. Too much thinking compounds drift, small errors multiplying with every extra step. Metacognition is the sweet spot: one honest check, most of the benefit, none of the drift, so it's this app's default wherever a depth has to be chosen.

Reasoning depth for "Why?" explanations

Metacognition drafts an explanation, then checks it against both sentences before showing it, one reflection cycle, no compounding multi-step drift.

Stakes (applies to both tabs below):

Works best as a specific claim to verify, not an open question. "Cats and dogs are both commonly kept as pets" gives a clean true/false signal; "what do they have in common" doesn't.

The model first drafts one specific, checkable claim from your question, Metacognition level, one reflection cycle, no open-ended phrasing left to disagree with itself over. That claim then goes through the same four checks.
