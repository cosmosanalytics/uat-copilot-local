iWhat this is

A step-by-step guide to three things in Visokio Omniscope: signing in with your Google account, building a workflow automatically by describing it to Ask the Workflow Ninja, and building a report automatically by describing it to Report Ninja. Each step tells you exactly what to do in Omniscope, then a small vision-language model (SmolVLM) looks at a screenshot of what's actually on your screen and tells you whether it looks like that step is done — the same engine and worker used by the UAT Outcome Accountability console. It only advises; you decide when to move on. Nothing is uploaded — the model and the screenshot both stay in this browser tab.

1Setup

Load the vision model once, then choose how it sees your screen. This only needs to be done once for all three guides below.

Runs fully client-side: a SmolVLM model via Transformers.js on WebGPU, loaded through vision-worker.js (must be deployed alongside this file). Progress through each guide is kept only in this browser tab's memory — reload the page and it resets. Nothing about your screen, your Google account, or anything you type into Omniscope is sent anywhere; the only network calls are the one-time model weight download (cached after first load) and whatever Omniscope itself does in its own tab.
