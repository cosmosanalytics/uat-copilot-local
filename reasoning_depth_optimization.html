1 The Classical U-Shaped Trade-Off Curve
Minimizing Total Epistemic Risk (d*)
Total risk is governed by a fundamental trade-off:
  • Left Side (Under-Thinking): High risk of incompetence. The AI makes shallow guesses and fails on nuanced edge cases.
  • Right Side (Over-Thinking): High risk of compounding drift. Multi-step reasoning chains compound subtle errors (0.9510 ≈ 60% accuracy).
The bottom of the curve is the Optimal Reasoning Depth (d*) where competence is maximized before drift takes over.
2 The Sweet Spot (Tier 3)
Metacognition
Why Tier 3: Metacognition dominates production systems:
vs. Tier 2 (Deliberation): Deliberation reasons step-by-step but does so blindly with no calibrated confidence or boundary checks.
vs. Tier 4 (Planning): Planning spawns recursive subgoals that cause exponential drift, requiring expensive continuous verification.
Bounded Single Horizon: Checks evidence before drafting, eliminating the risk of compounding drift across sequential subgoals.
Calibrated Tri-State Decision Gate:
• Answer (C ≥ τ)
• Hedge (Uncertain)
• Abstain (Conflict)
The 80/0 Rule: Metacognition delivers 80% of the cognitive horsepower of deep planning with 0% of the compounding multi-step drift, bound strictly to a single reflection cycle.
3 Dynamic Control Rules
Adaptive MPC
Three runtime rules that dynamically govern depth:
Rule 1 Stakes-Driven Ceiling
Cap depth by cost of error: Low-stakes → Reflex; Standard → Metacognition; High-stakes → Planning.
Rule 2 The "3-Step Leash"
Never plan >3 steps without a tool call. Tool outputs are deterministic and reset drift back to 0%:
Think 2 Steps → 🛠️ Tool Call → Reset Drift (0%)
Rule 3 Asymmetric Early-Exit
If step 1 entailment exceeds 0.98 with zero contradictions, exit immediately and skip tree-search.
Deterministic Anchors: Python sandbox (math/logic) • SQL (data) • Linters (code syntax) • Vector DB (facts).
Core Law: Intelligence without verification isn't safer—it is just more convincingly wrong.
Optimal System = High-Order Reasoner + External Ground Truth Tools
