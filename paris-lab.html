Watch the word Paris form, then change it

Follow one answer word, Paris, from the first layer to the last. Then try to change it the outside way, by editing the prompt or the dice, and the inside way, by switching off a part, pasting in another prompt's activity, or steering. Every try shows how far Paris moved.

  1. Watch it form: An open-weights model shows the chance of Paris at every layer.
  2. Change it from outside: Edit the prompt, add pressure or roll the dice, on a WebLLM chat model or on the open model.
  3. Change it from inside: Lesion, patch or steer, and see the formation curve bend.
  4. The scoreboard: Outside and inside edits side by side on the same model.

Honest label: two engines are used for a reason. WebLLM runs compiled chat models on WebGPU and returns words and probabilities, but it cannot show a model's insides. So the inside steps use a separate small open-weights model (SmolLM2, Qwen or GPT-2) run by code written for this page, which uses WebGPU for matrix math only after a self-test and otherwise the CPU. The two models are different, so compare outside and inside edits only in the scoreboard, which uses one model. Small models sometimes do not know a fact, and the page warns you. The model weights download from huggingface.co. Nothing you type leaves your device.
