The Anatomy of AI Intelligence

Where do agents keep what they know, and how do teams share it?

🧠🕸️🤖

1 · The core map: two questions, four boxes

⬅ ONE AGENT (Intra)  
  A TEAM (Inter) ➡
OUTSIDE LLM   |   INSIDE LLM
🔬 Inside + One agentNeural wiring: features and attention circuits firing in the model's layers. "What lights up when it thinks?"
📡 Inside + TeamModels sharing embeddings or latent signals directly. "Do they think alike?"
📝 Outside + One agentWorking notes: facts, hypotheses, tool results. Wrong beliefs get pruned (red dashed). "What do I believe right now?"
🤝 Outside + TeamWho hands off to whom, who checks whom, and the consensus bridge (green). "Who knows what?"

2 · "Is that all?" Three more dimensions

🌍
World ModelThe real environment (ledgers, ERP, customers). Tools are probes that fix an agent's beliefs when they drift from reality.
🎛️
MPC ControllerThe team's thermostat. Easy task? Shrink to one agent. Risky task? Grow a committee with checkers.
⏳
TimeOne run is a snapshot. Across thousands of runs the system must keep and compress what it learned.

3 · Where is memory? Everywhere, as 4 layers

📝
WorkingIntra-mind. The scratchpad right now.
📖
EpisodicWorld model. The diary of what happened.
📚
SemanticInter-team. Shared facts, who knows what.
🚴
ProceduralMPC. Habits and routing rules.
Memory × ( Intra-Mind × Inter-Team × World Model × MPC Control ) = Intelligence
Memory is not a fifth box. It is the thread that runs through all four, so the system gets smarter instead of starting over.
