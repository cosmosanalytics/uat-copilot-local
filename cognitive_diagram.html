The 6 Core Cognitive Aspects of an Autonomous Mind

How an intelligent agent plans, deduces, double-checks, remembers, empathizes, and innovates.

STAGE 01
Executive Function
"The Pilot"
Breaks big messy goals into step-by-step DAG task networks. Reroutes around errors and suppresses bad impulses.
➔ Plans & Directs the Mission
STAGE 02
Invariant Reasoning
"The Scientist"
Reduces problems to first-principles physics and math. Tests "What-if" failure cascades and deduces true root causes.
➔ Calculates Invariant Logic
STAGE 03
Metacognition
"The Mirror / Auditor"
Thinking about thinking. Runs an adversarial internal red-team to audit code, score uncertainty, and catch mistakes.
➔ Audits & Catches Own Errors
STAGE 04
Memory Systems
"The Archivist"
Compacts bloated context windows into clean scratchpads. Turns painful trial-and-error into permanent SKILL.md recipes.
➔ Compacts Context & Retains Recipes
STAGE 05
Theory of Mind
"The Empath / Telepath"
Reads between the lines to model what humans and peer agents actually need. Mediates multi-agent debates into consensus.
➔ Understands Unstated Human Intent
STAGE 06
Divergent Thinking
"The Artist / Maverick"
Escapes boring conventional answers. Takes wild lateral leaps and fuzzes extreme edge cases to discover breakthroughs.
➔ Breaks the Mold & Learns Anew
