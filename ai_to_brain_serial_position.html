🧠 1. Human Finding: The Serial Position U-Curve

In cognitive psychology (Murdock, 1962), humans recall the earliest items (Primacy effect: rehearsal transfers items to LTM) and final items (Recency effect: still active in short-term buffer) far better than middle items, producing an iconic U-shaped recall curve.

⚙ 2. AI Research Question: Does the Model Suffer "Lost in the Middle"?

Transformer architectures rely on bidirectional self-attention with RoPE positional encodings. Research (Liu et al., 2023) reveals LLMs exhibit an uncanny mirror of human serial position: high recall at token 0% and 100%, but a catastrophic retention trough in the middle 50%.
