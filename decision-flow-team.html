Decision Flow Team: AI agents that fill the grey areas of waiting

When you run 13 AI coding sessions at once, most of them sit idle, waiting for you. The bottleneck is not the AI — it is the queue of decisions only a human can make. This team of AI agents does everything possible to shrink that queue: it spots what is blocked, drafts a recommendation with evidence, assigns it to the right person, checks if it can be safely delegated, sets a deadline, and escalates if nothing moves.

  1. Intake officer: Reads the work item or decision request and extracts the key facts — what is blocked, who owns it, and why it needs a human.
  2. Three analysts: A risk analyst, a scope analyst, and a cost analyst each assess the decision from their own lens and produce a one-sentence verdict.
  3. Delegation checker: Decides whether the decision can be safely handled by rules (and what those rules are), or truly needs a human eye.
  4. Recommender: Drafts the shortest possible recommendation a human needs to say yes or no — no more, no less.
  5. Scheduler: Assigns urgency, an owner, and a deadline. Flags overdue items.
  6. Escalation agent: If a decision is overdue and still unresolved, proposes an escalation path and a default action.

Honest label: the agents are a small on-device chat model, so some replies cannot be parsed, and the page falls back to rules and says so. The decision pipeline logic (delegation check, urgency scoring, escalation) runs on plain code. Nothing you type leaves your device. The WebLLM model downloads from huggingface.co and is cached in your browser.
