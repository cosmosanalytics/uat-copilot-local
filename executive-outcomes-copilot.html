2Load a local AI model (WebGPU)

Downloads once (cached afterward) and runs on your device's GPU via WebGPU. The data above never leaves this browser tab — there's nowhere for it to go, since it's synthetic to begin with, but the same local-only design would hold for real data too.

3Generate executive actions

The model reads a digest of your KPIs plus the highest-revenue delayed orders, highest-value at-risk deals, and open critical support cases — including their free-text notes — and proposes a short, prioritized list of actions, each tied back to the records that motivated it.

On a phone or a laptop without a dedicated GPU this can take 1–3 minutes — the status line will keep updating with a live token count so you can see it's still working.

5How this works & its limits

The data is entirely synthetic. There is no real connection to SAP, Salesforce, or any other system — the tables in step 1 are generated in your browser from templates each time you click "Generate fresh synthetic data." This is a demonstration of the pattern (structured KPIs + free-text records + a local model → evidence-linked executive actions), not a live integration.
Everything runs locally. The model runs in this browser tab on WebGPU. If this were wired to real SAP/Salesforce data, that same local-only design would mean order notes, deal next-steps and case descriptions never leave the device doing the analysis.
Model disclosure. Recommendations come from a small open-weight instruction model (1–3B parameters), constrained to a fixed JSON output shape and given only a curated digest of the highest- risk and highest-value records, not the full dataset. Treat every action as a draft worth checking against the underlying records (click the evidence chips) before acting on it — not a verified conclusion.
