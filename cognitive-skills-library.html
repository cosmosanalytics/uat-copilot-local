💡 Why This Library Exists

LLMs don't fail because they aren't smart. They fail because raw prompts are like shouting instructions across a noisy kitchen!

A Skill is a recipe card with strict ingredients, numbered steps, and a test at the end to make sure nothing burned. When you package cognition as skills, models become dependable partners that plan ahead, double-check their math, and never hallucinate!
