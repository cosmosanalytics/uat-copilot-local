🧠 Biological Brain: Ebbinghaus Forgetting Curves

Hermann Ebbinghaus discovered that human memories decay exponentially over time: \(R = e^{-t / S}\). Forgetting is an active biological feature—not a flaw. Without synaptic pruning, the brain would drown in outdated noise, destroying retrieval speed and pattern recognition.

⚙ AI Architecture: Dynamic Vector Context Decay (Idea #3)

In autonomous agents, retaining every conversational turn indefinitely causes token bloat, high costs, and catastrophic retrieval poisoning. The Forget Slider simulates synaptic decay: inactive memories fade and are evicted from prompt context, while reinforced memories reset their retention curves.
