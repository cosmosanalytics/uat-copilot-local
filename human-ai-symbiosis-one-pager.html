3 The Plasticity Asymmetry: Why Neither is Complete Alone

👤 The Human Brain: Deep but Slow Plasticity

  • Slow Physical Remodeling: Biological neurons take weeks or months of repetitive practice and sleep consolidation to build new synaptic pathways (Long-Term Potentiation).
  • Working Memory Bottleneck: Humans can only maintain ~4–7 concepts in mind simultaneously before experiencing severe cognitive overload.
  • Prone to Bias & Forgetting: Without external records, biological memory decays, distorts past events, and carries legacy emotional friction.

🤖 The AI System: Fast but External Plasticity

  • Frozen Weights: The LLM neural network itself cannot change a single synapse during runtime; it has zero internal memory between API requests.
  • Zero-Latency External Rewiring: By updating external documents (Knowledge Graph, Vector DB, `SKILL.md`), the agent's behavior adapts instantly in the very next turn.
  • Infinite Working Breadth: Sweeps tens of thousands of tokens and entity links in milliseconds with deterministic precision.
