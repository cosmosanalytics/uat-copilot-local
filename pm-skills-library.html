➔

➔
Early Course Correction
🎯 Concrete Delivery Mission: Real-World Mission: Mid-Project Financial & Schedule Audit
"Project has spent $60,000 out of $100,000 budget. Team says they are 'almost done'."
  • Planned Value (PV): $50,000. Earned Value (EV): $40,000. Actual Cost (AC): $60,000.
  • Cost Performance Index (CPI): EV / AC = 0.67 (Critical budget overrun!).
  • Schedule Performance Index (SPI): EV / PV = 0.80 (20% behind schedule!).
  • Corrective Action: Descope secondary features immediately to prevent $50k blowout.
🔑
The PM Golden Rule: Projects don't go a year late overnight. They go a year late one day at a time. EVM catches Day 1.
