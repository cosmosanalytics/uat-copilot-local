🧠 1. Human Finding: Ebbinghaus Decay Curve

Hermann Ebbinghaus (1885) discovered that human memory drops precipitously: ~42% retained after 20 minutes, ~33% after 24 hours, and asymptotes around 21% without rehearsal. The forgetting curve follows an exponential power law governed by biological synaptic LTD.

⚙ 2. AI Research Question: Does an Agent Forget Over Distance?

We test whether an LLM's attention mechanism suffers a structural decay curve over conversational turn depth (token distance). Does needle recall degrade predictably like human memory, or exhibit sharp phase-transition cliffs?
