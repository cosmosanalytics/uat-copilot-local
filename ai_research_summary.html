AI Research Frontiers & Taxonomy

Executive overview: cognitive systems, embodied intelligence, scientific discovery, and next-gen architectures

Scope: Indexes 0 – 6  |  Format: Executive 1-Page Summary
Index Frontier Core Question Real-World Application
0 Language & Reasoning Systems
(System 1 / System 2)		 “Can AI think fast and deliberate step-by-step?” Reflex routing (Bespoke Nimble), coding agents, math proofs, in-browser WebGPU models
1 Physical AI & Robotics
(Embodied AI)		 “Can AI control a physical body?” Humanoid robots, factory automation, end-to-end self-driving cars
2 AI for Science & Discovery
(AI4Science)		 “Can AI discover new physical laws?” Cancer therapeutics, room-temp superconductors, fusion plasma containment
3 Multimodal AI & “World Models”
(Vision, Audio, Space)		 “Can AI understand 3D physics and time?” Interactive real-time game engines, cinematic video synthesis, spatial audio
4 Safety, Alignment & Interpretability
(Mechanistic Inspection)		 “Can we see inside the black box?” Safety auditing, bias removal, mechanistic neuron probing, constitutional AI
5 Breaking Past the Transformer
(New Architectures)		 “Can we make AI 100x cheaper and faster?” Million-token context (SSMs/Mamba), linear attention, optical/analog chips
6 Synthetic Data & Self-Play
(The “Data Wall”)		 “What happens when the internet runs out of data?” Self-play engines, formal verification, synthetic textbook generation & curriculum curation
