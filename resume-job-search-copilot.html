5How this works & its limits

Everything runs locally. PDF text extraction and the language model both run in this browser tab using WebGPU. Your resume text is never uploaded to a server, and no data leaves the tab, other than the one-time model download from a public model CDN.
Why this generates links instead of live results. LinkedIn's job search has no public, unauthenticated API, and scraping signed-in search results from a browser tab would violate LinkedIn's Terms of Service. So instead of pretending to fetch results, this tool has the local model do the part it's actually good at — reading your resume and proposing the job titles and keywords worth searching — and turns those into standard LinkedIn job-search URLs (title + boolean keyword query + your filters) that you open yourself, already signed in to your own LinkedIn account.
Model disclosure. Keyword and job-title suggestions come from a small open-weight instruction model (1–3B parameters) chosen for fast in-browser loading, not from a specialized resume model. Treat its suggestions as a fast first draft to edit in step 3, not a final answer — review and adjust the titles and keywords before searching.
