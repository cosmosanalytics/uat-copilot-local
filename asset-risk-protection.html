Asset Risk Protection Console

A first-principles model of CO₂ corrosion risk in a water or oil process system: temperature, pressure, flow velocity, and dissolved contamination combine to define a corrosion rate, expressed as distance to a warning and a danger boundary. Monte Carlo simulation turns today's operating point into a probability of crossing each boundary — the same logic behind routine monitoring vs. escalated inspection vs. intervention.

5Model disclosure

Model disclosure. This app is a simplified, illustrative model inspired by the published structure of CO₂ corrosion prediction approaches (de Waard–Milliams 1975; NORSOK M-506-style flow/temperature treatments): it reuses the well-established fugacity-corrected fCO₂0.62 driver, but the temperature/protective-scale/flow-stripping combination and calibration constant here were authored for this demo and tuned by hand to land in a realistic 0.01–5 mm/yr range. It is not a validated reproduction of any named standard and not for engineering design use — it exists to demonstrate the asset-risk-protection reasoning pattern: first-principles model → Monte Carlo → distance-to-boundary → tiered treatment.
