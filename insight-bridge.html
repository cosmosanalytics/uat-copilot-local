Remoteness band = Mednick's associative-distance theory of creativity: the best connections sit at a moderate distance, not the closest match. Three drafts, one picked = blind variation and selective retention (Campbell): generate several candidate ideas, then select. Cross-domain bridging itself = bisociation (Koestler) and literature-based discovery (Swanson's ABC model, the real technique behind the fish-oil–Raynaud's-syndrome finding).
