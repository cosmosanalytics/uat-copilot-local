🧠 1. Human Finding: 4-Chunk Capacity Barrier

In cognitive science (Cowan, 2001), human working memory maxes out at ~4 discrete chunks when concurrent manipulation is required. Asking a human to mentally resolve 8 intersecting logical variables without pen-and-paper causes near-100% executive failure.

⚙ 2. AI Research Question: Where Does the Model Break?

We test the model's working memory ceiling by feeding progressively deeper intersecting dependency graphs (4, 8, 12, 16 variables). At what load does the LLM suffer cognitive saturation, hallucinating bindings and violating constraints?
