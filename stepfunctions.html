AWS Step Functions (a real Amazon States Language interpreter)

Step Functions lab

Edit a state machine in Amazon States Language and run it. Task, Choice, Pass, Wait, Succeed and Fail states work, with Retry and Catch, InputPath, Parameters, ResultPath and OutputPath. Inject failures to watch retries back off and errors get caught.

Honest label: the interpreter here implements a practical subset of the Amazon States Language in JavaScript, and the Lambda functions are small local stand-ins. It does not call AWS, and it skips real waiting. Map, Parallel, timeouts, intrinsic functions and most service integrations are not implemented. Real executions are recorded by the service.
