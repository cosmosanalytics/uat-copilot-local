Healthcare Process Studio

A small local LLM orchestrates 10 tool calls — one per stage of a single patient's standard US insurance-based care journey (Registration → Eligibility → Prior Authorization → Clinical Encounter → Medical Coding → Claim Submission → Adjudication → Payment Posting → Denial Management & Appeals → Patient Billing). Each tool is a pure, deterministic function modeling the real math and gating rules of that stage (deductible/coinsurance/copay math, CPT/ICD-10 coding, prior-auth clinical criteria, denial/appeal outcomes); the model's only job is deciding which to call, in what order. Run stages manually to study the logic, or let the agent orchestrate the whole chain. Stage order and definitions are adapted from published revenue-cycle-management and utilization-management literature (see footer for sources) and simplified for illustration — this is not medical, billing, or legal guidance.

Planned service & prior authorization
Preauth is only required for imaging_advanced / surgery / inpatient_admission (a simplified prospective-utilization-review rule). office_visit_established never requires it.
Claim adjudication
The contracted "allowed amount" is what the payer recognizes as payable at all; the difference from the billed charge is a contractual write-off the provider absorbs — not paid by the patient. Set this to 0 to force a denial and exercise the appeals path.
Denial management — appeal
Only used if Adjudication results in a denial. Strength ≥ 0.6 overturns the denial on appeal (simplified); below that, the denial is upheld and the claim closes as a provider write-off with no plan or patient payment.
