How this works

This page downloads a small vision-language model straight from the Hugging Face Hub into your browser (once — it's cached after that) and runs every inference on your own GPU via WebGPU. Nothing is uploaded anywhere: your screenshots never leave the tab. Requires Chrome or Edge with a WebGPU-capable GPU. The first load can take a while depending on your connection and model size.
