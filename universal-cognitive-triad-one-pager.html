4 The Three Physical Laws That Force This Architecture

⚖️ 1. The Stability Paradox

If your compute engine rewired its physical weights on every fleeting sensory input, it would suffer catastrophic forgetting. Decoupling compute from external memory keeps the engine stable while remaining adaptable.

⏳ 2. The Bandwidth Bottleneck

No entity has infinite parallel bandwidth. You cannot compute all past history at once. The executive engine must act as a virtual memory manager, dynamically "paging in" only the 3 relevant facts for the current moment.

🛡️ 3. The Dual-Process Safety Rule

Fast pattern matching (System 1) is prone to impulsive hallucinations. Deliberate executive supervision (System 2) must intercept raw output and enforce strict safety guardrails before physical actuation occurs.
