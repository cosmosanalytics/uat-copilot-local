AI Factory lab

Try the AI Factory ideas hands on. Plan with MPC on a deterministic model, push a request through the centaur harness with its four checkpoints, run the full business request workflow, simulate factory scenarios, and consolidate decisions up the levels.

Honest label: the inventory MPC, the scenario simulation and the consolidation are real calculations in JavaScript on made-up numbers. The language model is a small open model running locally through WebLLM and WebGPU, and its confidence comes from token probabilities. The four checkpoints, the Control rules and the consolidation rules are simplified illustrations of the AI Factory ideas, not a formal implementation. Small models make mistakes, and the checks are there to catch them. Nothing leaves your device.
