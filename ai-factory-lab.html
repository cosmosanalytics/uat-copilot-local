A hierarchy of four agents (a real on-device model, simulated factory)

AI Factory lab

The AI Factory as a hierarchy of four agents. Decisions consolidate up, limits flow down, and the top is written by a human.

  1. Product operator: Handles each request, from clean to control, with a model and tools.
  2. Product designer: Consolidates the operator's decisions into a design signal and a calibration curve.
  3. Factory operator: Uses MPC scenario simulation to choose the product's configuration.
  4. Factory designer: Consolidates the factory operator's decisions into limits and defaults.

Honest label: the inventory MPC, the scenario simulation and the consolidation are real calculations in JavaScript on made-up numbers. The language model is a small open model running locally through WebLLM and WebGPU, and its confidence comes from token probabilities. The four agents are a framing: each one observes, decides, acts and reports, and only Level 1 uses the model to act. The four checkpoints, the Control rules, the design signals and the proposals are simplified illustrations of the AI Factory ideas, not a formal implementation. Small models make mistakes, and the checks are there to catch them. Nothing leaves your device.
