Symphony Composer Team: from brief to original symphony

Describe the symphony you want to hear — a mood, a story, an emotion. A team of specialist AI agents turns your words into an original three-movement work: they pick the key and scale, invent melodic themes for each movement, plan the harmonies, and three orchestrators realize it in chamber, romantic, and baroque style. You hear all three versions, pick your favourite, and download the WAV.

  1. Musical director: Reads your brief and decides the key, mode (major/minor/dorian), overall emotional arc, and tempo feel.
  2. Thematic composer: Invents a melodic character for each of the three movements — the shape, rhythm, and register of the main theme.
  3. Harmonic planner: Chooses the chord language for each movement — triumphant, stormy, lyrical, flowing or melancholy.
  4. Three orchestrators: Chamber, Romantic, and Baroque — each realises the same musical material with different instruments, dynamics and articulation.
  5. Sound critic, fixes and curator: Each take is measured, critiqued, fixed and ranked. You choose which interpretation to keep.

The score

Compose a symphony, then press Listen on a take.

Honest label: the agents are a small on-device chat model, so some replies may be unusable — the page falls back to structured rules and says so. The musical material is generated procedurally from the agents' semantic choices (key, contour, rhythm, harmony style): the code turns those into actual notes, synthesizes them with Web Audio, and renders offline. Nothing you type leaves your device.
